**Keep it professional.** Comments and annotations should stay respectful and professional. Treat restricted data as restricted, including screenshots and exports, and do not share them outside the group that holds the data.
Respect data access. Data you can see through a team/group you belong to, e.g. photometry, spectroscopy, analyses and comments, stays within that team/group unless the people who provided it agree otherwise. The same goes for anything you can see because someone shared it with you directly. Where data carries its own access terms those terms apply here as well.

**Share back.** If you take follow-up on an object in ICARE, register your telescope in the database following the instructions and record that you have the data, even if the data itself stays restricted to your group. Anyone who can see the object will see that follow-up exists and who to contact, but not the data itself, so that people know who to ask before duplicating it. If you need access granted, contact the ICARE team. Candidates surfaced through a group’s shared filters should be posted back to that group.

**Cite what you use.** Peer reviewed articles, AstroNotes, TNS reports and public presentations coming out of work done here should cite SkyPortal (van der Walt et al. 2019, Coughlin et al. 2023) and the alert broker (e.g. BABAMUL, BOOM, Fink, …) that supplied the data, along with the specific broker-filter, and should acknowledge the facilities and programs that provided the follow-up. A standard acknowledgement block you can copy is linked from the Source pages.
